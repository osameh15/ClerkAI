# BDR-005: Enterprise Revenue May Include Setup, Integration and SLA Fees

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | Accepted |
| Date | 2026-09 (Blueprint v1.0) |
| Deciders | Founder |
| Blueprint | BP §5.1, §5.2, §28.3 |

## Decision

Enterprise contracts may include setup/onboarding, migration, integration and SLA fees, and limited professional services for organization-specific templates and workflows. Technically, Enterprise terms are represented as contract-based **organization entitlement overrides** and custom PlanVersions; no Enterprise-specific code paths.
